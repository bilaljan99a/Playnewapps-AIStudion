Commission payments do NOT dictate our editorial opinions, product ratings, or review outcomes. We never accept payment to write positive reviews for low-quality apps or software. If a software tool fails our performance, privacy, or value standards during testing, we clearly highlight its drawbacks in our review pros and cons.
