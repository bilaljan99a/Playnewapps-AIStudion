In-Depth Review

Performance

On modern hardware, rendering speeds are comparable to mid-range laptops. Handling 4K footage at 60fps is incredibly smooth, especially on devices with M1 or M2 chips. We experienced zero crashes during our intensive 10-hour editing test involving multiple layers of 4K ProRes footage.

UI & UX Design

The interface is fully customizable. You can adjust the layout to prioritize the preview window, the timeline, or your media library. It's built ground-up for touch, though keyboard and mouse support on iPadOS makes it feel like a traditional desktop NLE.

Core Features

Advanced keyframing, LUT support, and precise audio mixing make it a powerhouse. The recent addition of Multicam sync is a game-changer for mobile journalism and vloggers, allowing you to sync up to 6 camera angles automatically.

Security & Privacy

Luma Touch is transparent about data collection. The app operates entirely locally, meaning your projects and media are not uploaded to any cloud service unless you explicitly choose to export them there. No accounts are required to use the software.
